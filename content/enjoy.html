Don’t panic.
Seriously – enjoy. Enjoy the channel, enjoy the conversation, enjoy using Java.
We’re all pretty easy-going, by and large (although we all have our days).
We generally have pretty active senses of humor (liberally applied to almost everyone), and we try to have fun.
We’re also pretty experienced, and we can help most users, somehow – even if it’s limited to redirecting to the appropriate communities, or empathizing with a specific problem.
So feel free to participate! We look forward to getting to know you.
(And if this page seemed saccharine-sweet, well… you’re probably not wrong. ron – one of the ops – wanted a happy, friendly, welcoming page, so we wrote one. We blame him, and so should you.)
